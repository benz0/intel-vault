# Review prep

1. **Read the accomplishments log first.** Then the feedback log.
2. **Cross-check the rest of the vault** (session logs, subject `Current State.md` files, internal notes) for things that happened but were never logged. Missing entries are common; say what you found and propose adding them.
3. **Group by what the audience cares about.** Ask the owner if unsure. For most reviews: results with numbers, then delivery, then helping others (tools built, people coached).
4. **Every claim carries a number or a date.** Strip anything you cannot source and list it separately as "believed but unsourced."
5. **Use the owner's own review format** if their local rules or career folder describe one.
6. **End with what is thin,** so the owner knows where the evidence gaps are before someone else finds them.
